# Loki III v1.0.0

Release notes, 2026-11-03. Loki III is part of Yggdrasil III (hub: <https://github.com/drpaj12/YggdrasilIII>).
Paper: `docs/paper/loki.tex` (see `docs/paper/README.md`). Every number below comes from the paper's
register (`docs/paper/hypotheses.md`) or the `EXPERIMENT.md` file named next to it.

## What Loki is

Loki III takes a structural netlist (gates, lookup tables or hard blocks) and tries to recover the
hierarchical, word-level design that produced it, without a language model. It measures how far it gets
against an answer key that Odin III provenance supplies. The base install needs only the `odin3` package.
Odin III is never changed.

## The pipeline

- **L0, ground truth.** Odin III provenance links each netlist object back to the source text. That link is
  the answer key: it labels all 54,306 gate nodes of 150 designs (rows L0a, L0b). Names exist only in
  `key.json`; the renamed-instance re-run over 105,646 identifiers shows the pipeline is naming-neutral
  (`experiments/rename-l2`). Commands: `loki bench`, `loki assemble`, `loki check`.
- **L1, Bayesian hypotheses.** Detectors, provers and a commit step, with calibration on the train split
  (`loki re`, `loki calibrate`). Calibration cuts the calibration error from 0.650 to 0.217 (L1c). Hard adders
  that synthesis already recovered are found 67 of 67 and hard multipliers 30 of 30 (L1h).
- **L2, graph analysis.** Graph export, structural features, clustering (k-means, affinity propagation,
  spectral, Louvain), graphlet orbit counts (ORCA, fetched and built separately) and a GNN baseline
  (`loki analyze`). `cell_type` is a label in `graph.json`. Clustering does not find the designer's
  hierarchy (L2a, DISMISSED).
- **L3, patterns and blocks.** A pattern catalogue with a v0 detector (`loki/l3/`) and the anchor-grow-join
  search (research code under `experiments/`).

## Detectors and block search

- FSM detection, including one-hot state machines: the encoding call is right for 97 of 98 one-hot FSMs
  (FSM2b, POSSIBLY GOOD). The version-2 FSM claims recover more FSMs but at low precision (FSM2a, DISMISSED);
  a next-state rule that tried to fix the precision was DISMISSED too (FSM3).
- Hard-block detection (`hard` detector): see L1h above.
- L3 v0 detector, measured per cell (rows L3A-*): the FSM detector works in four of five cells and the
  counter detector works on the Odin III hard-block target; the shift-register and timer detectors do not
  work (reported as measurements, not counted in the verdicts).
- Anchors, grow, stop, join (Q-L6 to Q-L9): blocks grown from anchors and joined by a conductance rule are
  the right block for 0.715 of 1141 held-out constructs, against 0.385 for bounded cones (QL9-a USEFUL;
  QL7a, QL7b USEFUL). The extra anchors (flip-flop banks, open feedback loops, constants) make 47 of 58
  target constructs right (QL8b-a, POSSIBLY GOOD). A split step and the Bayes-factor stop
  rule were DISMISSED (QL9-d, QL6a). The kind guess on the blocks reaches a macro F1 of 0.392, the weakest
  part (`experiments/ql8c-kind`).

## Three endpoints

The same 156 VerilogEval references are run at three endpoints: the gates endpoint (Gate-Breaker's own
cmos6 gate netlist, floor f3), the k4 endpoint (soft-logic lookup tables) and the k6 endpoint. Mean cells
per design are 305.3, 63.2 and 113.5; one k4 cell stands for 4.8 gates and one k6 cell for 2.7 (QL11-c,
`experiments/ql11-astcfg-why`). A construct is recovered more often when it is large, not less
(QL11-b, DISMISSED as stated; large 0.779, small 0.653 over all endpoints).

## Gate-Breaker replica and the language-model arms

`experiments/llm-pilot`, `llm-full` and `llm-hints2` replicate the Gate-Breaker harness (137 VerilogEval
problems, three draws) with arm A (netlist), B (netlist and Loki hints), C (interface line only) and the
follow-up arms A2, B1', B2 (linked hints v2) and W (wrong claims). `experiments/ql10-blocks` adds arm D
(Loki's joined blocks decoded one per prompt, a script assembles the RTL). No model call runs inside the
repository.

- The netlist helps beyond the interface line alone: B minus C is +0.319 [+0.241, +0.394] (LLM4, USEFUL).
- Version-1 hints do not help a model that has the netlist: A 0.888, B 0.878 (LLM3, DISMISSED).
- Version-2 linked hints B2 are the one Loki-plus-model arm ahead of the netlist alone: pass@1 0.901
  against 0.802 for A2, +0.099 (+0.052 to +0.151), on 64 problems (LLM5, QL11-a). Wrong hints (W) do not
  pull the model below A2 (LLM6, DISMISSED). AST and CFG similarity for B2 move by +0.001 and +0.007.
- Blocks arm D: pass@1 difference against A is +0.008 (-0.031 to +0.054), INCONCLUSIVE; AST and CFG
  similarity are slightly lower (-0.026 and -0.028; QL11-a, DISMISSED on those two measures). On the 27
  multi-block designs D passes 72 of 81 draws against 73 of 81 (QL10-a, INCONCLUSIVE). D runs where A
  cannot: 8 of 12 draws pass on designs too large for one prompt, and 954 of 969 block replies check
  (QL10-b, USEFUL). The kind-guess hint on blocks does not help (QL10-c, INCONCLUSIVE).

## Research rounds and verdicts

Every claim is a hypothesis with one experiment and one verdict (`docs/paper/hypotheses.md`). Of 110
register rows, 107 have a verdict or a measurement: 14 USEFUL, 12 POSSIBLY GOOD, 21 DISMISSED, 34
INCONCLUSIVE and 26 measurements (counts as of 2026-11-03, after Q-L11). Three hypotheses (L3, L6, L8)
are untested. Rounds: R1 to R6, HAL comparison (the word-first detector against DANA, INCONCLUSIVE), the LLM
rounds, and Q-L4 to Q-L11. Round records are in the paper appendix and under `experiments/`. Loki does not
detect Trojans (Leman Russ III does). No area, speed or power is measured.

## Known limits

- The language-model arms were run at the gates endpoint only. No k4 or k6 model replies exist.
- The swallowing hypothesis (a construct swallowed by a block that grows past it) is not tested beyond
  the join step; recovering swallowed constructs is POSSIBLY GOOD (QL9-c).
- The kind guess is weak (macro F1 0.392); the shift-register and timer detectors do not work (L3A-shift,
  L3A-timer).
- Clustering does not give the designer's hierarchy; the word-first detector lost to DANA (inconclusive).
- The paper's abstract still quotes 104 register rows; the register above (110) is current.
- Loki needs Odin III (`odin3` is not on PyPI); the analysis and GNN extras need numpy, scipy,
  scikit-learn, networkx and torch with torch_geometric.
